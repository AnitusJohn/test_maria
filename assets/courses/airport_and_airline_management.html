Your Gateway to a Career in Aviation

Airport and Airline Management is designed for students who are interested in building a career in the aviation, airport, airline, travel and hospitality sectors.

The course introduces students to the different departments and operations involved in the aviation industry, while developing communication, customer-service and professional skills.

Students can gain knowledge of airport operations, airline services, ground handling, passenger management, ticketing, reservation and air cargo.

  • Airport & Airline Operations
  • Cabin Crew & Passenger Services
  • Ground Handling
  • Ticketing & Reservation
