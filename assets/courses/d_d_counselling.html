Diploma in Guidance & Counselling

Diploma in Guidance &Counselling course is offered by MMTT meant for both aspiring and working professionals who are related to the field of Counselling. The course has been developed keeping in mind the various theories and concepts that today’s Counselling Professionals need to be aware, in order to establish themselves as a competent professionals.

This course includes the topic as various approaches beneficial in the field of Counselling – Freud’s Psychodynamic Approach, The BehavioralApproach, The Humanistic Approach, The Cognitive-behavioral Approach, The Solution-focused Approach, The Narrative Approach, The Feminist Approach and The Multicultural Approach, along with many other equally necessary skills, awareness and knowledge that an adept counsellor is expected to possess so that he/she may be able to help students better understand, and effectively deal with the challenges that they face in their lives. Leading a fulfilling life and not succumbing to the pressures of study and modern society are what counsellors strive towards for the students.

This course helps to provide leadership and services students enlargeto bring restoration to trauma survivors around the world.

Who can attend?

Fluent in English Leaders who have a calling for counselling Aspire to be a Guidance & Career Counselling School counsellor Mental Health Counsellor Struggling Parents in educating their children, Person who has desire to guide the children in Education Veteran Counselloretc…

Course Duration: Six Months

Age Bar: 21years and above

Eligibility: Graduation and Above

Course will be conducted by professionals who are practicing Counselling and Educationalists.
