Certificate on Teaching English as Foreign Language (TEFL)

TEFL involves teaching English as a foreign language in countries where English is not the first language. English is the most widely spoken languages in the world and used almost everywhere which is only one of the many benefits of learning English. In fact, learning English can surprisingly shape your life.

Through this Level 3 Diploma in Teaching English as a Foreign Language (TEFL), you’ll be able to improve in reading, writing and speaking skills. This course starts off with a brief explanation of grammar that makes it easier to understand English efficiently. Step-by-step arranged modules will help you on your way to becoming a prominent foreign language teacher.

COURSE CURRICULUM

Teaching in Different Situations: Needs and Constraints

Working With a Variety of Approaches

Teaching Speaking

Teaching Listening

Teaching Reading

Teaching Writing

Teaching Grammar

Lesson Planning and Testing
