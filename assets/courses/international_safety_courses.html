FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Safety Courses FAQ

What are Safety Management courses?
Safety Management courses introduce learners to workplace safety, hazard awareness, risk management, emergency preparedness and related safety practices.
What Safety courses are available?
The course areas include Fire & Safety Management, Industrial Safety Management, Environmental & Occupational Safety, Oil & Gas Safety and international courses.
What are IGC, IOSH and OSHA courses?
IGC, IOSH and OSHA refer to internationally recognised safety training and workplace safety frameworks or qualifications. Course availability and certification depend on the specific programme offered.
Which industries use safety professionals?
Safety practices are relevant across industries such as manufacturing, construction, oil and gas, energy, hospitality and other workplaces.
