Hotel Management FAQ

What is Hotel Management?
Hotel Management is a professional field covering hospitality operations, guest services, food and beverage, front office, housekeeping and administration.
What courses are available?
The available course areas include Hotel Management, Bakery & Confectionery, Food Production, Food & Beverage Service, Front Office Management and Hospitality & Hotel Administration.
Can I study Bakery & Confectionery?
Yes. Bakery & Confectionery is one of the hospitality course areas, covering bakery, pastry and confectionery fundamentals.
What career areas are available?
Students can explore hospitality environments such as hotels, resorts, restaurants, cafés, food services, guest services and hospitality administration.
