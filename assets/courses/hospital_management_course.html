FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Hospital Management & Paramedical Courses

What are Paramedical Courses?
Paramedical courses focus on healthcare support services and can introduce students to areas such as medical laboratory technology, operation theatre technology, radiology, imaging and patient care.
What is DMLT?
DMLT refers to Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology. The program focuses on fundamental laboratory procedures and diagnostic support practices.
What is Operation Theatre Technology?
Operation Theatre Technology introduces students to operation theatre procedures, equipment, sterilization, patient preparation and support activities.
What does Patient Care training cover?
Patient Care or Nursing Assistant training can cover basic patient support, hygiene, communication, comfort and assistance within healthcare environments.
Where can students work after completing healthcare-related courses?
Depending on the qualification and employer requirements, opportunities may exist in hospitals, diagnostic centres, laboratories, clinics and other healthcare organizations.
